Wholesale Cash Offer

Fast, as-is, no repairs on the seller. ARV x 75% − repairs − our fee.

—
Benefits & Requirements
Best fit when
  • Seller wants the fastest, simplest close possible
  • Speed and certainty matter more than top dollar
  • Can close quickly — often 7–14 days
  • No financing or lender contingencies to manage
Benefits to seller
  • No repairs, no showings, no financing risk
  • Fast close — seller picks the closing date
  • We handle all paperwork and closing costs

Equity Protection Program

We fund repairs, list on the open market, seller nets the difference. Adjusted comps x 90% − repairs − our fee.

—
Benefits & Requirements
Ideal seller situation
  • Not willing to take a low cash offer, but still motivated to sell
  • Wants convenience — a hands-off experience
  • Can wait roughly 60–75 days to close and get paid
  • If occupied: seller/tenants willing to give reasonable access for showings
Property condition
  • Move-in ready (rent-ready)
  • Dated but functional
  • No major repairs or rehab needed
Benefits to seller
  • Opens the buyer pool to cash investors and traditional buyers
  • We cover closing costs, commissions, and any repairs/updates
  • Seller gets exactly the agreed-upon net price
  • Hands-off, "5-star" experience — we handle inspections, negotiations, repairs, and closing
  • Typically nets a higher price than an all-cash offer
  • No agent commissions or paperwork hassle for the seller
